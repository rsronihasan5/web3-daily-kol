# Web3 KOL: Brian Armstrong (@brian_armstrong) — betting the future economy runs on AI agents

*2026-11-25 · post 3 of 3*

**Who he is:** Brian Armstrong is the co-founder and CEO of **Coinbase**, the largest US crypto exchange. His X handle is [@brian_armstrong](https://x.com/brian_armstrong).

**Why he's worth watching right now:** Armstrong is making the loudest bet in crypto on **agentic finance** — the idea that AI agents, not humans, will soon be doing most of the economy's transactions, and crypto will be the payment rails between them.

**The recent facts (verified):**

- **17 November 2026, TomorrowX conference in Austin (CoinDesk):** Armstrong predicted AI agents will account for **"well over 99.9%" of all transactions within a decade**, with crypto handling payments between agents. He also stood by his view that **Bitcoin could reach $400,000 by 2030**, and said prediction markets and derivatives now make up **25% of Coinbase's trading fee revenue**.
- **7 November 2026, on X:** Armstrong described how he actually works now — a **chief-of-staff agent overseeing seven or eight lead agents**, each running continuously with its own sub-agents, all feeding one prioritized decision queue. He later said each lead agent performs at the level of an **L5/L6 tech employee**, and that Coinbase runs roughly **1,200 full-time AI agents** internally, fed by a system called **Coinbase Brain**.
- **18 November 2026, Founders podcast:** Armstrong named **third-party vendors as one of Coinbase's biggest security risks**, said the company is replacing outside software with in-house tools, and noted Coinbase keeps **99% of the crypto it holds entirely offline** in cold storage.

**What builders can take from him:**

1. **Agents need money rails.** If agents really do the transactions, they need a way to pay each other — Armstrong's answer is crypto, and Coinbase is building for it (including x402, its agent-payment protocol).
2. **Delegation beats prompting.** His setup is not "ask the chatbot" — it's a persistent org chart of agents with a human review queue. That's a pattern any builder can copy at small scale.
3. **Security is the bottleneck.** Even while pushing agents everywhere, he puts 99% of holdings offline and audits every line of AI-reviewed code before deployment. Speed without security is how exchanges die.

Armstrong is not a trader or a meme KOL — he's a CEO building the infrastructure for the next economy, and right now he's betting that economy belongs to agents. Whether he's right about 99.9% or not, the direction is worth following.
