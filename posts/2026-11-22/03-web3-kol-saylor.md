*2026-11-22 · post 3 of 3*

**Michael Saylor** — [@saylor](https://x.com/saylor) on X — founder and executive chairman of Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy), the world's largest corporate Bitcoin holder.

Why he's the spotlight today: his company is living through the exact market this week's posts describe, and he's still buying.

Verified facts, all from this week:

- **Still buying on the dip.** Strategy's SEC filing on Monday 16 November showed it bought **485 BTC for $33.4 million** between 9–15 November, at an average of **$68,887** per coin — even as Bitcoin slid toward $62,000.
- **The stash is now 850,849 BTC**, bought for roughly **$64.2 billion** at an average price of **$75,452**. With BTC near $60,000, that treasury is about **$9.6 billion underwater** on paper.
- **He's not hiding from it.** On Sunday he posted a portfolio chart valuing the stash at $55.7 billion and wrote, "This dip could use a little orange." On 20 November he posted again, calling Bitcoin "the foundation of everything we do at Strategy."
- **The pressure is real, not just price.** MSCI confirmed this week it is moving to delist Strategy from its USA Index under new eligibility rules — Saylor has called the treatment "discriminatory."
- **He's also defending the balance sheet.** Strategy added a "USD Strength" metric to its public dashboard, showing its dollar assets can fund **3.4 years** of interest and dividends, and it spent **$228.1 million** buying back its STRC preferred shares in the same week.

What I take from Saylor as a builder: conviction is easy to post when the chart is green. The interesting part is what someone does when their own treasury is $9.6 billion underwater — and Saylor's answer, right or wrong, is the same as it has been since 2020: keep buying, keep explaining, keep building the machine. I don't have to agree with the leverage to respect the consistency.
